This is the area256 blog. We train language models from scratch on small budgets, and we'll write up what we find here.

Posts will cover full training runs, the experiments that didn't work, and the numbers behind both: loss curves, benchmark scores, and what each run cost.

Most posts will have more than one author, since most runs do.
